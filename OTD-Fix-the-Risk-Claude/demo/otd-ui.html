How the Agent Works
▼
Agent Skills
🔍
OTD Risk Detection
Scans open sales orders with delivery dates within the next 7 days. Cross-references inventory, production, delivery and freight signals to flag at-risk orders.
🔬
Root Cause Diagnosis
Classifies the primary delay cause: material shortage, production constraint, warehouse bottleneck, or carrier delay. Cites the exact SAP signals supporting it.
📊
Priority Scoring
Ranks orders by a weighted score combining customer tier (1–3), SLA urgency (days remaining), and revenue. Score 1–9 so planners focus on the highest-impact orders first.
📋
Advisory Dossier
Generates a structured per-order action plan: root cause, confidence rating, 3 concrete recommended actions referencing the correct SAP Fiori app, and a revised promise date estimate.
